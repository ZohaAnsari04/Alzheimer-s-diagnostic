# Ethics & Safety Protocol: NeuroPath AI

## Scope & Boundaries
- **Clinical Decision Support Only**: This platform is designed exclusively for patient prioritization and workflow triage.
- **Non-Diagnostic**: The system does NOT produce diagnostic findings, probabilities of Alzheimer's disease, or medical recommendations.
- **No Medication Recommendations**: No pharmaceutical or treatment choices are generated by the algorithm.

## Data Governance & Privacy
- **No Real PII**: Synthetic cohort generation uses pseudorandom IDs (`P-1001`, `P-1042`). No real names, addresses, or identifiers are collected or stored.
- **Audit Logging**: All record views, searches, data imports, and prioritization runs are logged into an audit trail.

## Human-in-the-Loop Mandate
All prioritization outputs and stage recommendations require explicit clinician review and validation.
